Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There's no account to create, no analytics, no advertising, and no server this app talks to — everything happens entirely on your device.

What's stored, and where

Vehicles, maintenance records, mods, fuel logs, and any photos you attach are stored locally on your device only. None of it is uploaded anywhere, synced to a cloud service, or visible to anyone but you.

Your rights & control over your data

Because nothing about you is collected or stored anywhere but your own device, there's nothing on a server for us to give you access to, correct, or delete on your behalf — you already have full control. Delete any vehicle, log entry, or photo directly in the app at any time, or uninstall the app to remove everything at once. This means rights frameworks like GDPR or CCPA (access, correction, deletion, portability) are automatically satisfied — there's simply nothing held anywhere else to act on.

Backups

The Backup & Restore feature in Settings creates a file on your device containing your data. That file only leaves your device if you explicitly share or move it yourself (e.g. via the share sheet, AirDrop, or a file provider you choose) — the app never sends it anywhere on its own.

Camera and photo access

If you grant camera or photo library access, it's used only to attach a photo to your own maintenance records, stored locally like everything else. Photos are never uploaded or transmitted.

Third-party services

This app doesn't use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and it doesn't share data with any outside company. The one exception is entirely your choice: the optional "report incorrect spec" feature hands off to your device's own Mail app (see below) — nothing is sent automatically.

Reporting an incorrect vehicle spec

If you use the "report incorrect spec" option, it opens your device's own Mail app with a pre-filled draft — the app hands this off to your mail client, then it's entirely your choice whether to send it. The app itself never sends this data automatically.

Notifications

Reminders (upcoming service heads-ups and occasional "haven't logged in a while" nudges) are scheduled locally on your device using iOS's own notification system. No account or external service is involved.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age — it doesn't collect information from anyone at all.

Questions about this policy

Questions about this policy or how your data is handled can be sent to hello.cmm@outlook.com.

Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated to reflect that before the change ships.
